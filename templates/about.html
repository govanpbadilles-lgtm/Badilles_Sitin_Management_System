About the System

Efficiently managing laboratory resources for the College of Computer Studies.

Our Purpose

The CCS Sit-in Monitoring System is a dedicated platform designed to streamline and modernize the laboratory access process for students. It replaces manual logbooks with a secure, real-time digital tracking system, ensuring that laboratory resources are utilized efficiently and fairly.

Our Mission

To provide the College of Computer Studies with a reliable, transparent, and user-friendly digital platform that modernizes laboratory management and enriches the academic experience of every student and faculty member.

Our Vision

A paperless, data-driven CCS laboratory environment where students are empowered through accountability, transparency, and fair access — fostering a culture of excellence within the University of Cebu community.

Key Features

  • Real-Time Monitoring: Admins can view active laboratory users instantly.
  • Session Tracking: Automatically deducts and manages the 30-session or 15-session limits based on the student's enrolled course.
  • Secure Authentication: Ensures that only registered and authorized CCS students can access the facilities.
  • Comprehensive Records: Maintains a detailed history of laboratory usage for reporting and analytics.

What We Offer

Session Tracking

Real-time sit-in logging with entry/exit timestamps and automatic session credit deduction.

Leaderboard

Gamified ranking system that highlights the most diligent and responsible laboratory users.

Lab Reservations

Students can pre-book laboratory slots, reducing crowding and ensuring equitable access.

Admin Reports

Comprehensive usage analytics and downloadable reports for laboratory administrators.

Feedback System

Students can leave feedback and interact with announcements, keeping the community in the loop.

Secure Access

Role-based authentication ensures data privacy and appropriate access levels for all users.
